526. A. A. BENNETT and C. A. BAYLIS. 1. A calculus for propositional concepts. Mind, n. s.
                vol.44 (1935), pp. 152-167. "An abstract deductive structure capable of interpretation not
                only extensionally . . . but also intensionally, as a calculus of unasserted propositional
                meanings. In this latter interpretation the calculus deals with those relations which hold
                on the basis of the structure and not of the significance or truth values of these proposi-
                tional meanings."
