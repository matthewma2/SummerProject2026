99. A. N. WHITEHEAD.
                     1. The algebra of symbolic logic. A treatise of universal algebra with applications, by
               Alfred North Whitehead, Cambridge, Eng., 1898, pp. 33-116.
                     2. Memoir on the algebra of symbolic logic. Amer.jour. math., vol. 23 (1901), pp. 139-
                165, 297-316. "The keynote of this memoir is the prominence given to three ideas, namely,
                that of the 'invariants' of a function of independent variables, that of 'prime functions of
                independent variables,' and that of the theory of 'substitutions' of independent variables
                for independent variables. This last idea connects the algebra with the theory of Groups,
                and opens out a large field for investigation in that direction."
                     3. On cardinal numbers. Ibid., vol. 24 (1902), pp. 367-394. Section III, pp. 378-383,
                is due to Bertrand Russell.
                      4. The logic of relations, logical substitution groups, and cardinal numbers. Ibid., vol. 25
                (1903), pp. 157-178.
                      5. Theorems on cardinal numbers. Ibid., vol. 26 (1904), pp. 31-32.
                      6. Note. Rev. mitaph. mor., vol. 13 (1905), pp. 916-917. "Je maintiensavec assurance
                que les id6es de l'Analyse sont capables d'etre exprim6es en termes logiques."
                      7. On mathematical concepts of the material world. Philosophical transactions of the
                of the Royal Society of London, series A, vol. 205 (1906), pp. 465-525.
                      8. La thlorie relationniste de I'espace. Rev. metaph. mor., vol. 23 (1916), pp. 423-545.
                      9. Mathematics, nature of. The encyclopaedia Britannica, 14th. edn., vol. 15 (London
                and New York 1929), pp. 85-89.
                      10. Logical definitions of extension, class, and number. Abstract by W. V. Quine. The
                 American mathematical monthly, vol. 41 (1934), pp. 129-131.
                      11. Indication, classes, numbers, validation. Mind, n.s. vol. 43 (1934), pp. 281—297.
                 See Corrigenda, ibid., p. 543.
                      See also A. N. Whitehead and Bertrand Russell 194.
