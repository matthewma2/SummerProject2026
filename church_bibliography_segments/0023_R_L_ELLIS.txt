23. R. L. ELLIS.
         *          1. Notes on Boole's Laws of Thought. The mathematical and other writings of Robert
              Leslie Ellis, M.A., ed. by William Walton, Cambridge, Eng., 1863, pp. 391-394. "Let 5
              be Shem, N Noah,/father, 5 son; N=fS,sf=\.           Eliminating/ between these two equa-
              tions, we get S = sN. Nothing can be simpler than this: but the symbols s, f, are of a dis-
              tinct nature from those employed in the Laws of Thought. For fA does not denote a
              species of A, but an idea standing in a different relation to it. The distinction between
              these two kinds of symbols becomes more manifest when we reflect that/ 2 is not identical
              with/, but denotes 'father of father,' or grandfather."
                    2. Observations on Boole's 'Laws of Thought.' Report of the fortieth meeting of the
              British Association for the Advancement of Science; held at Liverpool in September 1870,
              London 1871, Notices and abstracts, pp. 12-14.
                    See also Robert Harley 272.
