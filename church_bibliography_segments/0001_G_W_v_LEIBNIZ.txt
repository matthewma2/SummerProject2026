                       Of the above, all except 1 and 2 were unpublished until after Leibniz's death. They
                  are arranged in the order of their first publication (so far as known to the compiler). The
                  dates of composition are in many cases not certainly known. Apparently Leibniz's first
                 attempts at the actual construction of anything in the nature of symbolic logic are con-
                 tained in 13-21. The starred titles are all of later date and their chronological order is
                  probably 10, 11, 29, 6, 12. Cf. La logique de Leibniz d'apres des documents inedits, by
                 Louis Couturat, Paris 1901, xiv+608 pp. See also F. B. Kvet 221, Giovanni Vailati 1811,
                 Giovanni Vacca 975, and Karl Diirr 4162.
