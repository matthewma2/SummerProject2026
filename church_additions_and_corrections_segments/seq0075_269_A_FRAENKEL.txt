269. A. FRAENKEL.
         *       2. Der Begriff "definit" und die Unabhangigkeil des Auswahlaxioms. Sitzungs-
             berichle der Preussischen Akademie der Wissenschajten, Phys.-math. Kl., 1922, pp.
             253-257.'


               With the clarification of Zermelo's notion of a definite Eigenschaft and its consequent
         elimination as a presupposed intuitive idea, the Zermelo set theory may be said first to
         have attained a degree of exactness comparable to that of the system of Principia mathe-
         matica (11116, 1942). A method of doing this is indicated briefly by Fraenkel in 2692, is
         incorporated in a system of axioms for set theory in 269/, and later applied to the actual
         development of some topics in set theory ' n 26922. An alternative method is due to Skolem
         2475, 10. And see also J. v. Neumann 2992, S.
