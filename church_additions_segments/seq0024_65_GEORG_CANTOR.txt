65. GEORG CANTOR.
                   1.1. Letters to and from Dedekind (1899). Georg Cantor Gesammelte Abhand-
               lungen mathematischen und philosophischen Inhalts, ed. by Ernst Zermelo, Berlin
               1932, pp. 443-450. See also Anmerkungen, by Zermelo, ibid., p. 451.
                   A series of fundamental papers by Cantor on transfinite numbers and related topics
               do not fall within the scope of this bibliography. Nevertheless they are of interest
               here because of their historical influence on the development of symbolic logic. For
               these the reader is referred to Cantor's Gesammelte Abhandlungen just cited. See also
             . Contributions to the founding of the theory of transfinite numbers, by Georg Cantor,
               translated by P. E. B. Jourdain, Chicago and London 1915.
